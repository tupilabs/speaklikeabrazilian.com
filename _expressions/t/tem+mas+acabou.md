title: "Tem mas acabou"
permalink: "/t/tem+mas+acabou/"
definitions:
  - definition: |
      We foreigners hear this expression, at first they may get
      confused as it is used without any pause, or commas. In reality,
      it might be easier to understand if one imagines it as
      “tem; mas acabou”.
      
      It means: “We have it, but it is sold out/over”. This expression
      is very common in commerce when you ask for a product but the
      store does not have it in stock at the moment.
    example: |
      - Tem queijo fresco?
      - **Tem mas acabou**.
      - Ah, que pena. Quando chega mais?
