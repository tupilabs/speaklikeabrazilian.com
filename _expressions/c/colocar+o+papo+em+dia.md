definitions:
  - definition: |
      As in [“bater papo”](/b/bater+papo), here “papo” means a chat, conversation.
      The expression “colocar o papo em dia” means **to catch up with someone**.
      
      You would use it with someone you haven't seen in a long time, especially
      close friends.
    example: |
      - Vamos fazer um churrasco esse final de semana pra **colocar o papo em dia**?
      - Claro, eu tenho um monte de coisa pra te contar.
