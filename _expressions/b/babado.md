definitions:
  - definition: |
      The word “babado” in itself can be a conjugated version of the verb
      “babar”, to drool. But in informal conversation, it is normally used
      by women to describe a **big juicy gossip, a bombshell**.
    example: |
      - Traz o bolo e o café que eu tenho um **babado** pra te contar!
