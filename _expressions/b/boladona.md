title: "Boladona"

variations:
  - "bolada"
definitions:
  - definition: |
      When someone is “bolada” (feminine noun, or bolado, masculine) that person
      is worried or concerned about something. “Boladona” is the aumentative of
      “bolada”, emphasizing that that person is really worried.
    example: |
      - O que aconteceu com a Raquel?
      - Ela viu umas mensagens no [zap](/z/zap) to [boy](/b/boy) dela,
        e ficou boladona.
      - [Babado](/b/babado)!
